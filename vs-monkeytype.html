freetypingtest.app vs Monkeytype: Free Typing Test Alternative

Monkeytype is one of the most beloved typing test tools on the internet, and for good reason. It's open source, beautifully designed, deeply customizable, and backed by a passionate community. If you're a typing enthusiast who loves tweaking every setting to perfection, Monkeytype is an outstanding choice.

freetypingtest.app takes a different approach. Instead of maximum configurability, it focuses on maximum simplicity. No settings to learn, no account to create, no decision fatigue. Open the page, start typing, get your results. Both tools are free and both measure your typing speed accurately — they just serve different kinds of users.

How does Monkeytype compare to freetypingtest.app?

Monkeytype shines with its extensive customization system. Hundreds of themes, adjustable caret styles, smooth animations with configurable smoothness, custom fonts, language support, and advanced statistics make it the go-to choice for the typing community. Its leaderboard and account system let you compete globally and track your progress across devices.

freetypingtest.app is built for people who want a clean, instant typing test without a learning curve. There's no settings page to configure — you pick a duration, pick a mode, and start typing. It includes features that matter most: real-time WPM tracking, a WPM-over-time graph, personal bests, test history, a dedicated code mode, and dark mode. Everything runs locally in your browser with no account and no data sent anywhere.

When should you choose freetypingtest.app?

When should you choose Monkeytype?

If you're a typing enthusiast who enjoys fine-tuning your experience, competing on leaderboards, and exploring community themes, Monkeytype is hard to beat. Absolutely. Many people use freetypingtest.app for quick checks and casual practice, and Monkeytype for deeper practice sessions. Since both are free and browser-based, there's no reason to limit yourself to one. The best typing test is the one you'll actually use consistently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is freetypingtest.app better than Monkeytype?

Neither is objectively better — they serve different needs. Monkeytype is the power-user choice with deep customization, hundreds of themes, and advanced statistics. freetypingtest.app is designed for simplicity: no learning curve, instant start, and a clean minimal interface. If you want maximum configurability, choose Monkeytype. If you want to just start typing immediately with zero setup, choose freetypingtest.app.

Does Monkeytype require an account?

No, Monkeytype does not require an account for basic use. However, creating a free account unlocks features like saving your history, accessing leaderboards, and syncing across devices. freetypingtest.app saves everything locally in your browser with no account needed at all.

Can I type code on freetypingtest.app like Monkeytype?

Yes, freetypingtest.app includes a dedicated Code mode with programming snippets in JavaScript, Python, CSS, HTML, and SQL. Both tools support code typing practice, though Monkeytype offers additional programming language options through its extensive customization system.

Which has more customization options?

Monkeytype has significantly more customization options, including hundreds of themes, adjustable smoothness settings, custom fonts, layout options, and many more configuration toggles. freetypingtest.app deliberately keeps customization simple with font size, dark mode, sound effects, and test duration and mode selection.
