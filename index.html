What is a good typing speed?

The average typing speed is around 40 WPM. A speed of 60-80 WPM is considered above average and sufficient for most professional work. Speeds above 80 WPM are considered fast, and professional typists often reach 100+ WPM. The world record for typing speed is over 200 WPM.

How is WPM calculated?

WPM (Words Per Minute) is calculated using the standard formula where every 5 characters count as one word. Gross WPM equals total characters typed divided by 5, divided by minutes elapsed. Net WPM subtracts errors from the gross WPM to give your effective typing speed.
